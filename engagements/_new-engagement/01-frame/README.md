# 01 — Frame

**Phase objective:** establish the engagement charter and Layer 0 (Strategy) content.

**Typical duration:** 1–2 weeks.

## Inputs
- Client brief, RFP, sponsor interviews.
- Practice account knowledge.

## Outputs
- [`engagement-charter.md`](engagement-charter.md) — signed by executive sponsor and practice partner.
- Compliance overlays selected and version-stamped in §7 of the charter.
- Engagement-specific pillar weights set in §8 of the charter.

## Checklist before exiting Frame

- [ ] Outcome statement is single-paragraph and stated in client language.
- [ ] Success criteria (≤ 5) are measurable, with owners.
- [ ] Executive stakeholder matrix lists named individuals with decision and veto rights.
- [ ] Scope boundary is unambiguous, with explicit exclusions.
- [ ] Compliance overlays selected and recorded (with version).
- [ ] Pillar weights sum to 100 and are negotiated with the sponsor.
- [ ] Charter signed by sponsor + practice partner + lead architect.
